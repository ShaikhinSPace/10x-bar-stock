10X Bar · Carbon study

This re-skins the stock app as a Carbon product: the dark UI Shell header, IBM Plex Sans and Mono, the 2x grid, data tables for anything with more than one fact per row, tiles for summaries, tags for status, and a single deep royal blue pulled straight from Carbon's Blue ramp — Blue 60 for every interactive surface, Blue 80 for brand. Partials like 0.25 and 1.87 stay first-class in Plex Mono. Semantic colour is reserved: red for out-of-stock and wastage, teal and purple only to tell the two bars apart.

SYSTEM   IBM Carbon  ·  TYPE  IBM Plex Sans / Mono
THEMES   Gray 10 (light) · Gray 100 (dark)
SCOPE    Visual study only — no application logic
SCREENS  Dashboard · Stock · Delivery · Activity · Manage
